# Visual direction

## Agreed direction
Japanese mountain roads, believable nature, smooth terrain and a visually polished car game. See [[References/Japanese Mountain Reference Board]].

## Proposed first scene
A fictional mountain pass inspired by Nikko and Hakone: a forested sweeper, a rock cut on the inside, a steel guardrail on the outside, and a distant valley visible through a gap in the trees.

Use late-summer vegetation and soft morning light as the first consistent look. These are proposed defaults, not user-approved season or lighting choices. The reference photographs show several conditions; use each for its labeled purpose rather than mixing every season.

## Material and shape rules
| Element | Target | Reject in the finished scene |
|---|---|---|
| Road | Continuous curved silhouette; worn charcoal asphalt; subtle texture scale | Visible rectangular stepping; giant texture tiles |
| Markings | Consistent thin painted lines with restrained wear | Oversized, glowing stripes |
| Road edge | Narrow shoulder; drainage where needed; terrain meeting the road | Floating asphalt; exposed gaps |
| Forest | Uneven clusters, varied height and rotation, understory | Identical trees at equal intervals |
| Rock | Weathered forms partly embedded into hillsides | Cubes stacked on slopes |
| Guardrail | Repeated posts, continuous rail, plausible height relative to car | Huge barriers that hide the valley |
| Distance | Several overlapping ridges fading into haze | One abrupt wall at the map edge |
| Lighting | Readable shadows, muted greens, soft depth haze | Bloom that washes out the asphalt |

Suggested palette: asphalt #34383A, deep cedar #263E32, moss #647354, rock #777C78, aged rail #A4AAA8, haze #B8C9CD. These are starting art swatches, not mandatory material values.

## Composition
Foreground: road, shoulder, grass and guardrail.
Middle distance: forest, exposed slope, a bend that invites driving.
Background: ridge layers, a valley opening, restrained sky.

The driver's view takes priority. Scenery should frame the next turn without hiding its entrance.

## First visual slice
Dress approximately 150–250 studs of one existing test-road section after road/suspension verification. Include one sweeper, one rock cut, one guardrail stretch, three tree silhouettes, ground cover and a valley vista. Keep the existing driveable test area available.

Capture a driver view, a roadside view and an elevated overview. Review with [[Visual Review]] before expanding.

## Asset policy
Source or model car bodies, trees, rocks, rails and tileable materials. Record origin and permitted use. Label temporary geometric assets as placeholders. Photographs are reference material, not textures to extract for the game.
