# Cars and upgrades

## Agreed feature
Players choose among different car platforms, modify their cars and save personalized builds.

## Proposed starter roster
Use fictional names and original branding. These are archetypes, not approved assets.

| Platform | Character | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cedar coupe | Moderate weight, rear-wheel drive, balanced power | First prototype and accessible starter |
| Ridge roadster | Short wheelbase, light weight, quick response | Agile technical driving |
| Summit coupe | Longer wheelbase, heavier, more torque | Broad sweepers and sustained slides |

Differentiate weight, wheelbase, steering and power delivery. No single platform should be best at every circuit.

## Performance tuning
Tires and grip; springs and dampers; steering angle; differential behavior; gearing; engine power; ride height within a useful range.

Offer sensible presets before exposing every numeric setting. Show each upgrade's handling effect in plain language. A change in wheel appearance should not silently change tire grip.

## Cosmetic customization
Paint, liveries, wheel designs, body kits, spoilers, interior accents and garage decoration. Expand only when assets exist.

## Saved build concept
Each build records car platform, installed upgrades, tuning values, cosmetics and a version. Validate compatible upgrades and tuning limits on the server when this feature is implemented.

## Development scope
Now: one test chassis and configuration values.
Next: one finished body and stable drift handling.
Later: garage interface, multiple cars, tuning presets and saved builds.

Technical implementation belongs in [[../PLAN|PLAN]], after the prototype is verified.
