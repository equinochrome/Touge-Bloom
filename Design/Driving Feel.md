# Driving feel

## Goal
Believable weight and motion with forgiving controls. Players should enjoy holding and linking slides on a keyboard, then have room to improve.

## Proposed first car
Rear-wheel-drive coupe with predictable power delivery. One controller and one test chassis first; appearance comes after stability.

## Tuning priorities
1. Stable rest and smooth suspension over road seams.
2. Clear steering response and progressive traction loss.
3. Controllable sustained drift, with visible weight transfer.
4. Smooth left-to-right transitions.
5. Recoverable over-rotation and a quick reset.
6. A chase camera that shows the slide and the approaching corner.

Keep countersteer assistance and steering smoothing tunable. Avoid a camera that snaps to every steering change.

## Playtest observations
- Can a new player start a slide without a tutorial?
- Can the car hold a 30–50 degree drift through a long sweeper for roughly five seconds?
- Does steering correction feel understandable?
- Is recovery possible without a full reset?
- Are keyboard transitions smooth?
- Is the behavior acceptably consistent at 30 and 60 FPS?
- Is the next corner visible throughout a slide?

The numeric targets come from the current plan and are provisional tuning goals. Record player feedback as well as telemetry.
