# Japanese mountain reference board

These official tourism photographs are external visual references. Images need internet access and may change at their source. The captions below are proposed art interpretations, not specifications of every real road.

## 1. Irohazaka / Nikko — the mountain-road setting

![Irohazaka mountain road reference](https://res-4.cloudinary.com/jnto/image/upload/w_750%2Ch_503%2Cc_fill%2Cf_auto%2Cfl_lossy%2Cq_auto/v1514185038/tochigi/Tochigi955_2.jpg)

[Source: JNTO — Irohazaka Winding Road](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/301/)

Study the relationship of winding pavement, forest coverage and mountain slopes. Reinterpret the landscape in a fictional circuit. Do not copy the entire road layout or impose narrow public-road dimensions on drift gameplay.

## 2. Lake Ashinoko — scenery opening

![Lake Ashinoko scenic reference](https://res.cloudinary.com/jnto/image/upload/v1/media_national_parks/filer_public/f8/be/f8be2a16-d16e-4f10-ab8a-29a5890c0dec/fuji-hakone-izu_26-thumb-280xauto-73942_u2n1jx)

[Source: JNTO / National Parks of Japan — Ashinoko-Hakone Skyline Drive](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/fuji-hakone-izu/see-and-do/ashinoko-hakone-skyline-drive/)

Use as inspiration for one scenic break in dense forest. A lake belongs to a coherent valley setting; it need not appear beside every turn.

## 3. Mikuni Pass — distant mountain reference

![Mount Fuji from Mikuni Pass reference](https://res.cloudinary.com/jnto/image/upload/v1/media_national_parks/filer_public/29/67/29678a23-b5c0-4dc5-9ab6-ecd832009eed/fuji-hakone-izu_27-thumb-280xauto-73943_f5vigz)

[Source: JNTO / National Parks of Japan — Ashinoko-Hakone Skyline Drive](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/fuji-hakone-izu/see-and-do/ashinoko-hakone-skyline-drive/)

Study distant silhouettes and the sense of a larger world. Use original ridge shapes. A recognizable Mount Fuji landmark is optional and not part of the first prototype.

## 4. Lake Ashinoko Tenbo Park — overview composition

![View from Lake Ashinoko Tenbo Park reference](https://res.cloudinary.com/jnto/image/upload/v1/media_national_parks/filer_public/20/6e/206e76b8-a411-4f3f-8754-3c287e10966a/fuji-hakone-izu_28-thumb-280xauto-73944_mbrex0)

[Source: JNTO / National Parks of Japan — Ashinoko-Hakone Skyline Drive](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/fuji-hakone-izu/see-and-do/ashinoko-hakone-skyline-drive/)

Use for the feeling of a destination overlooking a valley. Apply this to a pull-off or garage approach later, while keeping driving sightlines clear.

## 5. Hakone forest and cedar reference collection

[Source: JNTO / National Parks of Japan — Hakone landscape itinerary](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/fuji-hakone-izu/see-and-do/itineraries/experience-landscapes-unique-volcanic-terrain-hakone-where-histo/)

This page describes cedar-lined historic routes and moss-covered forests. Proposed application: varied tall tree silhouettes, darker understory and mossy ground near shaded slopes. The historic stone path is a landscape reference, not the driving surface.

## What still needs close-up references
- Asphalt grain and paint wear at car scale.
- Steel guardrail posts and ends.
- Drainage channels, shoulder transitions and retaining walls.
- Original fictional car proportions and wheel fitment.

Do not invent a detailed production asset from these distant landscape photographs alone. Add close-up references as assets are selected.
