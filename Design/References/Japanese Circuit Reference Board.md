# Japanese Circuit Reference Board

Collected 2026-10-10. These are real-venue references for fictional seeded circuits, not licensed replicas. Course diagrams are layout references, not construction blueprints. No surveyed GIS/elevation data has been acquired.

[[Japanese Mountain Reference Board]] · [[Japanese Circuit Generation Brief]]

## How to read this board

Separate observed facts from the proposed game interpretation. Never infer exact height, banking, road width or scale from a perspective photograph. Photos/maps remain private research references; do not upload them as game assets. Use our existing stud measurements for playable geometry.

## Ebisu North / West

[Source](https://www.ebisu-circuit.com/course.html)

Official separate course diagrams and photos. North and West should be separate inspiration profiles; use the PDFs to study corner rhythm and terrain relationships. Do not use historic South asphalt footage as the current South course.

Proposed game profiles: plateau sweepers for North; longer flowing loop with braking hairpins for West.

## Nikko Circuit

[Source](https://www.nikko-circuit.jp/base114.html)

Official guide distinguishes pre/post February 2018 layouts. Use the revised layout. Local visuals come from the event organizer https://challenge-road.net/nikko-cimage/ because direct official download had a certificate failure.

Proposed profile: flowing linked corners, a faster sweep and a recovery straight; modest elevation rather than invented alpine terrain.

## Minami Chiba Circuit

[Source](https://www.minamichibacircuit.com/course2.html)

The acquired diagram is explicitly from 2018. It is historical inspiration; present configuration and exact dimensions remain unverified.

Proposed profile: accessible compact practice loop, low elevation and clear runoff; scale tight turns up to the game minimum radius.

## Gunsai — Gunma Cycle Sports Center

[Source](https://www.gunsai.jp/facility/)

Correct venue name: Gunma Cycle Sports Center. Its facility guide shows a forested cycling route used for motorsport events. The illustrated map is not a surveyed engineering drawing.

Proposed profile: forested mountain loop, continuous grades, linked bends and restrained hairpins. This is the primary mountain-road inspiration.
