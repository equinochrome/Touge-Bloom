# Visual review

## Compare the same three views
1. Driver / chase-camera view approaching the sweeper.
2. Roadside view showing the car, rail and road scale.
3. Elevated view showing the road embedded into the landscape.

Store screenshots in `Design/References/Attachments`. Name each with the date, scene or seed, viewpoint and revision.

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] Road has a continuous curved silhouette with no visible segment steps.
- [ ] No floating road, exposed gaps or abrupt terrain intersections.
- [ ] Car, road markings, guardrails and vegetation share believable scale.
- [ ] Trees form varied clusters rather than repeated rows.
- [ ] Terrain has smooth forms and blended ground materials.
- [ ] Driver can see the next turn.
- [ ] Distant terrain has layered silhouettes and atmospheric depth.
- [ ] Lighting keeps the asphalt and car readable.
- [ ] Placeholder assets are identified.
- [ ] Asset provenance is recorded.
- [ ] Driving remains smooth after decoration.
- [ ] Performance was measured on the intended device, with results recorded.

## Feedback format
**Reference:** link to the specific photo or note.
**Screenshot:** embed the actual Studio capture.
**Mismatch:** describe one visible problem.
**Change:** specify the next adjustment.
**Result:** capture the same viewpoint after the change.

Example: trees are equally spaced and all the same height; create varied clusters with gaps while preserving the runoff zone.
